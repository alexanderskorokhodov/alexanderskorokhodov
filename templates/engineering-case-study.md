## Business problem

Describe the actual workflow, stakeholders, existing constraints and baseline. Separate observed problems from hypotheses. Do not invent buyer demand.

## Before and after

Explain inputs, decisions, human checkpoints, system outputs and the change in the workflow. Include a rights-cleared conceptual diagram if it helps.

## Architecture and technical decisions

Explain components, interfaces, data flow, integration boundaries, why important decisions were made and relevant trade-offs. Keep proprietary algorithms, prompts and source code private.

## Quality, safety and reliability

Describe actual tests, validation, failure handling, human oversight, security boundaries and monitoring. Distinguish verified controls from proposed controls.
