# Publication policy

## Purpose

This public repository helps prospective customers and engineering teams evaluate relevant work, technical judgment and the ability to deliver reliable software. Public material is documentation, not a distribution of commercial products.

## What may be published

| Material | Boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Project descriptions and case studies | Approved public facts, high-level architecture, workflow and limitations |
| Results and metrics | Traceable scope, units and measurement context; no invented outcomes |
| Demonstrations | Approved synthetic or otherwise authorized inputs and redacted outputs; no private implementation |
| Engineering notes and research | Original or correctly attributed material, with evidence status and relevant sources |
| Visuals | Rights-cleared, checked for identifiers, secrets and confidential details |

## What stays private

Commercial pilot source code, proprietary implementation logic and prompts, production configuration, credentials, client datasets, private logs, contracts and unpublished internal research must not be published here. A public description or demo does not grant access to the underlying product, its code or a commercial license. Third-party material retains its applicable terms; do not imply permission to redistribute it.

## Evidence and readiness

Label each case as historical project, deployed system (only when evidenced), prototype, or concept/research. Distinguish a published architecture description from verified runtime behavior. Distinguish a measured result from a target or an estimate. Record relevant limitations; omit unsupported claims. Do not present a prospect as a customer or a demo as an accepted pilot.

## Review before publication

- [ ] Owner has cleared any new substantive disclosure; client and third-party rights are respected.
- [ ] Facts, attribution, units, status and scope are checked against authorized evidence.
- [ ] Screenshots, metadata, files, diagrams and links contain no confidential information.
- [ ] No source code or proprietary implementation from commercial pilots is included.
- [ ] Links resolve; relevant limitations and the next contact or evaluation step are clear.

Keep private drafts out of all public Git history, including branches and pull requests. If a disclosure is suspected, stop publishing and notify the owner before taking corrective action.
